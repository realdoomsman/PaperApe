Quick start guide

Getting started

Follow the the guide to get started

After selecting your preferred platform, find a coin to trade. The trading interface will open automatically when you are on a coin page

Trading Interface

  • Quick buy buttons for common amounts
  • Percentage-based sell buttons
  • position info displayed above the sell buttons

Trading Settings

you can access the settings by clicking the settings icon in the top right corner of the trading widget

  • Priority Fee: usually higher fee means faster transactions. recommended value is 0.01-0.0015SOL
  • Slippage Limit: Maximum allowed price movement during a transaction
  • custom buy/sell amounts: dont like the default amounts? change them to your liking
  • custom execution time: dont like how long it takes for your trades to go through? You can set the transaction time by your self

Trading Wallet

Your wallet starts with 3 Solana. Use this virtual balance to practice trading without risking real money.

  • View your balance at the top of the widget
  • Click the wallet icon to add more SOL to your trading wallet
  • Reset your balance anytime from the wallet tab
  • Small preview of your stats with the ability to reset the stats and copy them, see the Trading History & Stats slide for more info

Trading History & Stats

Track your trading performance and analyze your stats with MockApe's analytics tools.

  • Navigate to the wallet tab to copy your trade history
  • Paste the copied data on mockape.com/stats to analyze your performance
  • See your activity via pnl calendar
